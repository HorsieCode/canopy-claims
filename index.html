Canopy Claims

Claim real-world plots and grow a glowing low-poly forest.

  • Walk to earn Hydro-Kinetic Energy (HE): 1 meter = 1 HE.
  • Plant on an empty plot next to you (yellow). The seed rolls a species: Ironwood Oak 50%, Biolume Redwood 35%, Amber Willow 14%, Dew Cypress 1%.
  • Water your trees with HE. 100% hydration = Canopy Bloom (2.5x) for a while, 1–99% = normal, 0% = Withered (0.1x).
  • Trees make Sap all the time, even while the app is closed.
  • The global engine multiplier is always 20x. Sap value: 1 Sap = $0.00000001 (100,000,000 Sap = $1.00).
  • GPS needs an https page and location permission. Tap Use GPS to switch back from Sim.
  • When GPS is weaker than ±50 m, the phone's step counter earns HE instead (0.76 m per step). Turn on Precise Location for best results.
  • Sim mode lets you walk with arrow keys / WASD or the on-screen pad (for testing).
