GeoSweeper

Minesweeper on the real world. Walk to a tile to dig it up.

  • You can only dig the tile you're standing on or one right next to it (highlighted in yellow).
  • Tap a tile to dig. Long-press, right-click, or switch to 🚩 mode to place a flag.
  • Tap a number whose mines are all flagged to dig its neighbours at once.
  • Hitting a mine costs a life and 50 points. You get a life back every 2 minutes.
  • Every player gets the same minefield, so you can compare scores.
  • Sim mode lets you walk with the arrow keys / WASD or the on-screen pad.
